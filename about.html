About Me

Abdulrhman Mohamed

Analyst and trader with more than 8 years of experience in global financial markets. I look at markets from a practical angle: how to understand a decision, measure risk, and review an idea after the outcome appears.

My interest in markets started from actual trading and watching how price behavior changes when economic and financial conditions change. Over time, I became more focused on turning observations into testable hypotheses instead of treating the market as a story or a quick impression.

I focus on building structured readings that combine macro context, the financial condition of companies or currencies, and price behavior. The goal is not to deliver ready-made answers, but to create a clearer framework for evaluating decisions before taking risk.

I write and build tools from the perspective of someone who works inside the market and understands that being wrong is part of the process. A market idea only has value when it can be reviewed, measured, and improved.
