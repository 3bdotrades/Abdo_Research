About

Abdulrhman Mohamed

Analyst and trader with more than 8 years of experience in global financial markets. I turn practical market experience into reviewable research and trading tools that help traders and portfolio owners evaluate decisions before execution, without return promises or ready-made calls.

What I Do

I build research hypotheses that start with the macro backdrop, then the financial condition of companies or currencies, then price behavior. An idea does not enter the workflow until it can be measured and reviewed.

How I Think About Risk

Risk is not measured only at the trade level. It also depends on the position and the portfolio as a whole. Trade size, invalidation level, and exposure limits are part of every research view.

Who I Write For

The work is built for discretionary traders, systematic traders, and active portfolio owners who want a more structured way to compare markets and scenarios.

Work Boundaries

Everything published here is for research and education only. I do not provide money management, personal recommendations, or outcome guarantees. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

1. Form the Hypothesis

Define a clear question: what behavior are we measuring, and in which market or asset does it appear with enough clarity?

2. Test and Review

Review the idea across historical data and different periods while separating development and validation data wherever possible.

3. Define Risk Rules

Convert the reading into a decision range that includes trade or position size, invalidation level, and exposure limits.

4. Publish and Monitor

Publish the research or video, then review it as conditions change instead of treating it as a final market verdict.

Covered Markets

Each market is treated as its own environment, with different data quality, liquidity, and risk conditions.
